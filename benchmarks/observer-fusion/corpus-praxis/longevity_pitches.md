### The Solution (60 words)

Praxis/Anti-Illusion Notebook — an open-source, self-hosted, data-dense biometric tracking system that treats the human body as embedded hardware requiring first-principles debug. 45 kg reversal, psychiatric medication elimination, documented via continuous biometric logging with delusion-removal rules (R1–R5).

### The Ask (40 words)

$75,000 for: (1) Rust backend productionization, (2) Svelte minimalist UI, (3) 6-month clinical replication by 3 independent subjects, (4) Open-source release under MIT license + data portability guarantees.

### The Metric (20 words)

3 replicated reversals of ≥15 kg metabolic decay + documentation of psychiatric medication elimination protocol, all data published openly under user ownership.
